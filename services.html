Questions

Good to know

Can I order just one piece?

Yes! There's no minimum order. Order a single keepsake or enough for a whole crowd.

Do I need a 3D file?

Not at all. A sketch, a reference photo or a few words about your idea is enough to get started. We handle the 3D modelling.

Can you personalise a piece?

Many of our pieces can carry a name, date or logo. Tell us what you have in mind and we'll let you know what's possible.

Can I choose the colours?

Most pieces can be printed in the colours you choose. Photos are for reference, and printed colours may vary slightly.

How long will it take?

Lead time depends on the product and the quantity. We'll confirm a timeline with your quote, so tell us if you have a date in mind.

Do your prices include tax?

Yes. All prices are per piece in Indian rupees and include 18% GST.
