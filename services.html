Caring for your pieces

Keep it cool

PLA softens above about 55°C. Avoid hot cars, dishwashers and strong summer sun.

Easy to clean

A soft, damp cloth is all it needs. Skip harsh cleaners.

Not made for food

Our pieces are not meant for food, unless we say otherwise.

Questions

Good to know

Can I order just one piece?

Yes! No minimum order. One gift or a whole event, we have you covered.

What are your pieces made from?

Plant-based PLA. Made from renewable crops like corn and sugarcane, not petroleum.

Do I need a 3D file?

Not at all. A sketch, a reference photo or a few words about your idea is enough to get started. We design and prototype custom pieces beyond our catalogue.

Can you personalise a piece?

Yes. Names, dates, initials or logos on pieces marked Custom: Available.

Can I choose the colours?

Match a theme, a party palette or your brand colours. Photos are for reference; printed colours may vary.

How long will it take?

Lead time depends on the product and order quantity. We'll confirm a timeline with your quote, so tell us if you have a date in mind.

Do your prices include tax?

Yes. All prices are per piece in Indian rupees and include 18% GST.
